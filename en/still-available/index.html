For appointment businesses

“Any openings Saturday?” Answered and booked.

Nails, lashes, barbers, massage: neuri.bots offers your open times, books the appointment and reminds your client. You keep working.

  • Offers open times

    When someone asks if you have room, it offers 3 open times from your schedule and books the one they pick. Never a taken one.

  • Your services and hours

    Add each service with its length and price, your hours per day and your lunch break.

  • Reminds them

    It reminds your client a day before and the same day, in the same chat.

  • Changes and waitlist

    They change or cancel by chat. If the day is full, it adds them to a waitlist and offers any spot that opens.

  • Deposit with your link

    If you want, ask for a deposit with your own payment link. The money goes straight to you; you mark when it arrives.

Good to know

  • We email you about every new, changed or canceled appointment, and you can add appointments by hand in My assistant.
  • Reminders go out on Instagram or Messenger within the 24 hours Meta allows after your client’s last message. WhatsApp coming soon.
  • The direct Meta connection is pending approval; meanwhile we set up your schedule and email you when it opens.

Frequently asked questions

Could my account get blocked?+

Neuri connects through Meta’s official API, not tricks or shady apps. The direct connection with Instagram and Messenger is pending Meta’s approval; we’ll email you as soon as it opens.

What if it gets something wrong?+

It only answers from your sheet. If a buyer asks something it doesn’t know, it doesn’t make things up: it passes you the conversation and tells the buyer you’ll reply. You can pause it anytime.

What do I need to get started?+

Your Instagram account or Facebook Page, and a sheet with your items or services, prices, pickup or delivery, and payment options. Sign-up takes 3 minutes.

Can I try it first?+

Yes. We give you a test code so you can message it from your personal account and see how it answers before turning it on for your buyers.

What about WhatsApp?+

It’s on the way. We’re opening Instagram and Messenger first (pending Meta’s approval); as soon as WhatsApp is ready, we’ll let you know so you can connect it.

Can I cancel?+

Yes, anytime. It’s billed month to month with no contract.

Do you charge per message or per contact?+

No. You pay the same flat monthly price no matter how many buyers message you: there’s no per-contact or per-message fee. For safety, each account answers up to 1,000 messages a day.

Who helps me if something breaks?+

In My assistant, tap “Something’s not right”: it checks your Instagram, your sheet and whether it’s paused, and gives you a button to fix each one. If it still doesn’t work, it goes to a person at Neuri and we reply by email.

What about Marketplace’s own auto-reply?+

Facebook already has an auto-reply for “is this still available?” inside Marketplace. neuri.bots answers on your Instagram and on your Facebook Page’s Messenger: prices, delivery, holds and your link for people who comment, using the info in your sheet. If you only sell on Marketplace from your personal profile, Facebook’s reply may be enough.

Why not use Instagram’s built-in auto-replies?+

Instagram’s built-in replies greet a first message and send an away message, and they’re free: if that’s all you need, use them. neuri.bots answers each different question with the info in your sheet (still available, price, delivery, holds) and DMs your link to people who comment on your posts.

Do I get a receipt or invoice?+

Yes, a receipt: once billing starts, every payment has a receipt you can download from the Stripe billing portal, which you open from My assistant. Neuri LLC is a US company; we don’t issue Mexican CFDI invoices yet.

What happens when the free month ends?+

A week before, you get an email to add your card; nothing is charged until the month ends. If you don't add it, your assistant pauses on its own a few days later. No surprise charges.

Is anyone using it yet?+

We’re opening with our first 10 sellers. That’s why setup and the first month are free: all we ask in return is your honest feedback.
