Common questions

Do my vendors have to download an app?+

No. They get alerts by email and check their sales in My Booth from their phone's browser.

My inventory is already in a spreadsheet. Do I have to enter it all again?+

No. Upload the whole thing from Excel or CSV and it's loaded in one go.

Do you take a cut of each sale?+

No. You pay one flat monthly price, no matter how much you sell.

How do I collect booth rent?+

Each booth has a fixed rent. It comes out of the vendor's balance automatically every week, and everything they sell is theirs. If their sales don't cover it, the difference carries over to their next payout.

Who can use the register?+

You decide. There are roles for owner, manager, cashier and staff, and each one only sees what they need.
