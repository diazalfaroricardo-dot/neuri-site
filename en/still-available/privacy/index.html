neuri.bots privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Version 1.11 (in force since November 26, 2026). neuri.bots is a service of Neuri LLC (Phoenix, Arizona, USA) that answers, on behalf of a business, its customers' messages on Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram, text messages (SMS, once available) and its website chat: whether an item is still available, prices, holds, appointments and orders.

1. Who is responsible

This notice is issued under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP), published in the Diario Oficial de la Federación on March 20, 2025 and in force since March 21, 2025, and also considers the US laws that apply to Neuri LLC.

  • Neuri LLC is the controller for the data of businesses that sign up (owners) and of visitors to neuri.tech. Address: 5818 W Raymond St, Phoenix, AZ 85043, USA. Privacy contact: hola@neuri.tech.
  • Each business is the controller for the data of its customers (the people who message it). For that data Neuri acts as a processor: it handles it only to answer on the business's behalf and according to its settings. If you messaged a business that uses neuri.bots, you can exercise your rights with that business or email us and we'll pass it on.

2. What data we handle

From people who message the business (Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp)

  • The text of the messages you send the business and the replies sent to you (we keep up to 1,000 characters per message).
  • The ID assigned by Meta (on Instagram and Messenger) or your WhatsApp number (wa_id). We use it to reply; in our message log it is stored as a one-way hash, not the ID or number. Exception: if the business offered to hold an item for you, or you asked about its price or availability, and you stopped replying, we keep the raw ID together with the item only while a single follow-up message is pending (at most 24 hours, the window Meta allows). Once it is sent or dropped, the ID is deleted and only a marker with the hash, the item and the date remains, so we don't message you about the same thing again. It is not sent if you asked us to stop messaging you.
  • Your profile name, only if Meta includes it with the message.
  • If you comment on a post or mention the business (for example, to get a link by private message): the text of the comment or mention and its ID.
  • Comment assistant (only if the business turns it on): we read every new comment on its Instagram, Facebook and TikTok posts, and the thank-you messages you send, to decide whether to like it, reply with a short “thank you!”, send you a Google review link once, hide spam or alert the business. Almost everything is decided with rules; only comments that are unclear are sent to an AI model (Anthropic) to classify them, without your name or username. We keep, for 90 days at most, a summary of what the assistant did (the start of the comment, up to 140 characters, an irreversible key (hash) instead of your ID, and the action) so the business can review and undo it. A complaint, a money matter or a health matter is never answered in public: it goes to the business. We never offer anything in exchange for a review.
  • If you ask not to be messaged (“stop”): that flag, so we don't reply to you again.

From people who write through Telegram or the website chat

  • Telegram: the text of the messages and the chat ID Telegram assigns; in the log it is stored as a hash, just like with Meta. Telegram processes the messages on its servers under its own terms.
  • Website chat: the text you type and a random ID your browser keeps in the business site's local storage to continue the conversation. We don't use cookies or ask for your name. We use your IP only to limit messages per hour.

From people who text the business (SMS)

Applies once text messaging is available for the business.

  • Your phone number and the text of the messages you send to the business's number and of the replies (up to 1,000 characters per message), to reply to you on the business's behalf. Twilio receives and sends those messages.
  • If you called the business's number, weren't answered, and the business turned on “Missed call”: your number and the time of the call, to send you a single text asking how they can help (at most once every 24 hours).
  • If you reply STOP: that flag, so we don't text you again from that number until you reply START.
  • Mobile phone numbers and text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties, except the providers that need it to deliver the messages (Twilio and mobile carriers).

Appointments

If you book an appointment by chat: the service, day and time, status (confirmed, awaiting deposit or canceled), the network you used and, if you join a waitlist, the day you wanted. If the owner logs an appointment by hand, they may type your name. Any deposit is paid through the business's own payment link: Neuri does not receive or see payment details.

Orders

If you place an order by chat: the items, quantities, total, pickup or delivery, the delivery address you give, notes (“no spice”), the time it will be ready and its status. Payment is made to the business; Neuri doesn't process it.

From the business using neuri.bots (owners)

  • Sign-up data: your name, email, WhatsApp, business name and type, hours, where and how you deliver, payment methods, FAQs and what the assistant should answer.
  • Connections: your Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp Business number ID and the access tokens Meta provides, stored encrypted.
  • If you connect Google Calendar or your Google Business Profile: see section 4.
  • If you link a Google Sheet: the published link of your catalog or menu sheet (we read it as CSV) and, if you paste it, your Apps Script web app link so we can send you orders and leads. That app runs in your Google account; we have no access to your Google Drive.
  • Plan billing: Stripe stores your card; we only receive the subscription and payment status.
  • Support and audit log: what you ask us from My assistant and the log of changes made to your account.
  • Team: if you invite other people (Owner, Admin or Member), their email, role, invitation and acceptance dates and the key of their personal link. Removed right away from “Team”.
  • Browser notifications: if you turn them on, your browser's subscription, up to 5. The notification goes through your browser's service (Google, Apple or Mozilla).
  • Catalog from Instagram: if you import it, we read the text and link of your last 30 posts; an AI model suggests items that you approve one by one.
  • Gift codes: if you receive a code to use a plan free or at a discount, we keep the code, who it was given to, when it was redeemed, for which business and whether it is still active, to control its use.
  • Affiliate program (applies once the feature is available): if you arrive through an affiliate's link (?ref=), we store our own sd_aff cookie with the affiliate's code for 60 days, only to know who to credit for your sign-up. It doesn't store your name or follow you on other sites.

Features turned on in stages

Applies once the feature is available for the business.

  • TikTok: we read the business's recent videos and the top-level comments from the last 48 hours. We store the comment text, its ID and the reply. The token is stored encrypted and deleted on disconnect.
  • Reviews on your Google Business Profile (business.manage permission): we read reviews from the last 14 days to thank 4–5 star ones and leave the rest as drafts. Details in section 4.
  • Business context files ("Train" in My assistant): if you upload a PDF, Word, Excel or CSV file, a photo (menu, price list, policies) or your website link, we extract its text and Anthropic's Claude model reads it (including text in photos) to suggest FAQs, facts and policies. We don't keep the file: it is processed in memory and discarded; we keep only its name, type and size and the suggestions. Your assistant uses only what you approve. Deleting the file deletes everything learned from it; deleting your account deletes everything. Don't upload your customers' personal data: if we detect it, it isn't suggested. From a link we only read the public page you paste.
  • AI support call: answered by an AI voice agent (Twilio and ElevenLabs) and we tell you so at the start. If it is recorded, we ask for your consent at the start.
  • Ad measurement with Meta (Conversions API): if the business turns it on, for chats that came from an ad in the last 7 days we tell Meta when they end in an appointment, hold, order or sale (with the amount, if known). We only send identifiers Meta already assigned (the ad-click ID or the conversation ID), never your phone number, name or email, not even hashed. It lets the business see which ads bring in sales.
  • Cal.com: if the business books with Cal.com, we check its open times and, when you book, we create the booking in its Cal.com with your name and, only if its event requires it, your email or phone. If the appointment is canceled, we cancel it there. Cal.com keeps it under its own terms.
  • Shopify: if the business connects its Shopify store, we read prices and stock. If you ask “where is my order?”, we ask for your email and order number to look it up, and we only reply with the status and tracking if the email matches the order (at most 5 lookups per hour). The store token is stored encrypted.

Sensitive data: we don't ask for or look for sensitive data. Please don't send it by chat; if it arrives in a message, it is only used to reply and is deleted with the message log.

3. Why we use it

Purposes needed for the service

  • Answer, on behalf of the business, questions about availability, price, location, delivery and payment methods; hold items; book, change or cancel appointments; take orders.
  • Notify the owner (by email and in My assistant) of holds, appointments, orders and conversations they should handle in person, and send orders and leads to their sheet if connected.
  • Send appointment reminders on the same channel, within Meta's rules (on WhatsApp, outside the 24-hour window, only with approved templates).
  • Bill the plan, provide support, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations.
  • Learning from your replies: when the assistant didn't know something and you answered by hand, Claude turns the pair into a general question and answer and suggests it to you; before sending it we remove emails, phone numbers and the buyer's name, and nothing is added without your approval.
  • Support: Support emails are answered first by an AI agent (Claude, by Anthropic) using only published information; billing, personal data, complaints and legal matters are reviewed by a person.
  • Your Neuri network: If you use several Neuri apps with the same confirmed email, we recognize you as the same person to show you “Your Neuri network”. Across apps we only match the owner's email, which apps are active and aggregate figures; never your customers' data.

Optional purposes (you can say no)

  • Send owners weekly and monthly reports, the satisfaction survey and the keyword summary. Every such email has an unsubscribe link; opting out doesn't affect the service.
  • Measure, in aggregate, how many messages are answered and what it costs, to improve the product.

We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising or use it to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Google data (Calendar and Business Profile) and Google's user data policy

Google Calendar

If the owner connects Google Calendar, we request only two permissions. We don't request calendar.events or calendar, so we can't read or change the events in their other calendars.

  • calendar.freebusy: see only the busy blocks (start and end) of their primary calendar. We don't see titles, descriptions, guests or any other event data. Those blocks are replaced each time they are read again; they keep the assistant from offering times when the owner is busy.
  • calendar.app.created: create a separate calendar called “Citas de Neuri” in their account and write one event there for each appointment the assistant books (service, customer name or short code and “Appointment booked by your Neuri assistant”); we delete it if the appointment is canceled. This permission can't touch any other calendar or event.

The Google token is stored encrypted; the access token lives only in memory. It is never included in the data download or in logs. You can remove access from My assistant or in your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.

Google Business Profile (reviews)

Applies once the feature is available for the business. If the owner connects their Google Business Profile, we request a separate permission, on its own Google screen: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage. We use it only to:

  • Know which profiles are theirs: we read the names of their accounts and locations and whether each location is verified. We only work with verified locations the owner manages.
  • Read new reviews: for reviews from the last 14 days that don't have a reply yet, we read the star rating, the text and the reviewer's name.
  • Reply to them: 4- or 5-star reviews with no complaint get a fixed thank-you, without AI, with the reviewer's first name (at most 20 a day per business). Reviews with 1 to 3 stars or with a complaint become a draft in My assistant and we let you know; they are never posted on their own: they go out only when you approve or edit them. We never offer anything in exchange for a review: a reply that offers discounts, gifts or refunds is not posted.

We keep only what is needed: the Google token, encrypted (the access token lives only in memory), the name and ID of the locations, pending drafts (star rating, review text up to 2,000 characters, first name and the suggested reply) and the IDs of reviews already handled, so we never reply twice. Review text is not sent to any AI model. You can remove access in your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps or with the Disconnect button in Connections (My assistant), or ask us to disconnect it at hola@neuri.tech. The button asks Google to remove the permission and deletes what we stored for that connection (token, locations, drafts and handled reviews); deleting your data deletes it too. Replies already posted stay on your Business Profile.

Limited Use

neuri.bots' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically: Google Calendar and Google Business Profile data is used only for the scheduling and reviews you see in My assistant; it is not transferred to third parties (including Anthropic or any AI model) except as needed to provide those features (our hosting and email providers), for security, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets; it is not used for advertising, not sold and not used to train AI models; and no person reads it unless you allow it, for security (investigating a bug or abuse), or as required by law.

5. AI-written replies

What the fixed rules don't cover is drafted by Anthropic's Claude model, acting as our service provider: it receives the customer's message and the business data needed to answer (catalog, hours, policies). Under its commercial terms Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. By default, the first reply to each person says it's an automated assistant, and you can ask to talk to someone at the business; with businesses in the United States that notice always appears (“You’re chatting with an automated assistant for <business>”). If you upload context files in "Train", Claude also reads them to suggest what your assistant should know; only what you approve is used and the file isn't kept.

7. How long we keep it

  • Message log: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Single follow-up: the raw ID, at most 24 hours; the hashed marker that we already messaged you, 12 months.
  • Reused replies: a general answer to a repeated question is kept at most 24 hours, without the question text and without personal data.
  • “Stop messaging me”: honored while the log exists; if you write again after 30 days, or write “start”, the assistant replies again.
  • Google Calendar busy blocks: replaced on each read and deleted on disconnect.
  • Google reviews (once available): each draft, until you approve it (at most the latest 50 are kept); IDs of reviews already handled, the latest 500; the token, until you disconnect or delete your data.
  • Appointments and orders: while the business has its account (the latest 500 orders and 200 waitlist entries are kept), so the owner can look them up.
  • Business data: while it has its account. If it cancels, data is kept so it can come back, until it asks for deletion (section 8) or until 12 months after canceling. About 30 days before, we email you the date; if you sign in before then, nothing is deleted and the period starts over. When it is up, the data is deleted just as if you had asked.
  • Backups: 7 daily copies of account data (without the message log), replaced automatically.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep (such as billing records) is kept only for the legal period; what Stripe keeps about payments lives at Stripe.

8. How to download or delete your data

  • If you messaged a business on Instagram or Messenger: remove the app from your Facebook or Instagram settings (Settings → Apps and websites). Meta sends us the deletion request and we delete the messages tied to your account across all businesses; you get a confirmation code and a page to check the status.
  • If you wrote on WhatsApp (or prefer not to use Meta's option): email hola@neuri.tech and we'll do it by hand. To stop the assistant replying, just write “stop”.
  • If you own a business: in My assistant you can download all your data as a JSON file (no keys or tokens) and disconnect Google Calendar (Meta and WhatsApp are disconnected by removing the app from Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business, or by emailing us). After canceling, you can delete everything from My assistant: sign-up, settings, message log, appointments, orders and connections with their tokens. Only one anonymous line (hashed code, dates and plan) remains for accounting.
  • Deleting your account (business owner): ask in My assistant → Account → “Delete my account and data” or email hola@neuri.tech from your account email. We send a confirmation link, only to your account email, that expires in 48 hours; nothing is deleted unless you click it. Once you confirm, your plan ends at the end of the period you already paid (Stripe doesn't charge you again and there are no refunds) and your data is deleted 30 days later, just like “delete everything”: sign-up, settings, message log, appointments, orders and connections with their tokens. Until that date you can change your mind with the link in the email and nothing is deleted. We keep only what the law requires: your payment records at Stripe and an anonymous line with dates (a record of the request, with an encrypted fingerprint instead of your name or email).

9. Your rights (ARCO) and how to use them

You can request Access to your data, Rectification, Cancellation (deletion) or Opposition to its use, and you can revoke your consent or limit how your data is used (for example, stop receiving report emails).

  • How: email hola@neuri.tech with your name, a way to reply, what you're asking for and, if you're a customer of a business, which one and on which network you messaged it. We may ask you to verify your identity (or your representative's).
  • Timing: we reply within 20 business days and, if it applies, carry it out within the next 15 business days.
  • If you're a customer of a business: the business is the controller. You can ask it directly or email us and we'll pass it on right away.

Authority (Mexico): since March 21, 2025, the former INAI's functions over personal data held by private parties belong to the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (Ministry of Anti-Corruption and Good Governance). If you believe your request wasn't handled, you can go to it.

10. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS); Meta, WhatsApp and Google tokens are stored encrypted; the IDs of people who message are stored as hashes in the message log; My assistant links are signed and expire; and only the Neuri team can open the admin panel, which keeps an audit log. If a breach significantly affects your rights, we'll tell you without delay.

11. If you're in the US

  • We don't sell or “share” personal data for targeted advertising.
  • If there's a data breach, we'll notify affected people under Arizona law (A.R.S. § 18-552, within 45 days) and other applicable state laws.
  • Commercial email under CAN-SPAM: reports, the survey and summaries identify Neuri LLC, include our postal address and a one-click unsubscribe link, and we honor opt-outs within 10 business days (in practice, immediately). Service emails (billing, connection, appointment and order notices) keep arriving.
  • neuri.bots is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13; we don't knowingly collect their data.

Your US state privacy rights

This part applies if you live in California or another US state with a consumer privacy law. The sections above explain what personal information we collect, where it comes from, why we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

  • We don't sell or share your personal information. We don't sell personal information, “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for profiling that has legal or similarly significant effects. We don't seek sensitive personal information; if it reaches us in a message, we use it only to provide the service. We haven't done any of these things in the last 12 months.
  • Your rights. Depending on your state, you can ask us to tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy in a portable format; correct it; delete it; and opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling (we don't do these, but you can still ask). We won't treat you differently for using these rights.
  • How to ask. Email hola@neuri.tech with the subject “Privacy request” from the email on your account, or use the download and delete options in section 8. We may ask you to confirm the request from that email to verify it's you. You can use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof that they act for you. We answer within 45 days; if we need more time (up to 45 more days), we tell you why.

  • Global Privacy Control. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a request to opt out of sale and sharing.
  • Data we handle for a business. If you messaged a business that uses neuri.bots, we handle your data as that business's service provider. Send your request to the business; if you send it to us, we pass it on.

Under attorney review.

12. Visits to neuri.tech and sd.neuri.tech

To know how many people visit the site and your dashboard, which pages help and which sign-up or payment step they reach, we use PostHog (US servers) in anonymous mode: no cookies and nothing stored in your browser, your IP address is not used to identify you (only to estimate the country), no screen recording, and it is never used for ads. We count visits, button clicks and which sign-up step someone reached, without your name, email, phone or anything you type. If your browser has “Do Not Track” turned on, nothing is counted.
