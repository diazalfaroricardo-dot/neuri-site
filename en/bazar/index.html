    Common questions

    Do my vendors have to download an app?

    No. They get alerts by email and check their sales in My Booth from their phone's browser.

    My inventory is already in a spreadsheet. Do I have to enter it all again?

    No. Upload the whole thing from Excel or CSV and it's loaded in one go. Or send it to us and we load it for you.

    Do you take a cut of each sale?

    No. You pay one flat monthly price, no matter how much you sell.

    How do I collect booth rent?

    Each booth has a fixed rent, weekly or monthly. It comes out of the vendor's balance automatically, and everything they sell is theirs. If their sales don't cover it, the difference carries over to their next payout.

    Who can use the register?

    You decide. There are roles for owner, manager, cashier and staff, and each one only sees what they need.

    Can I bring my data from Ricochet, Liberty or a spreadsheet?

    Yes. Import an export from Ricochet or Liberty, or any Excel or CSV file. Or send it to us and we load it for you.

    Does the register add sales tax?

    Yes. Set your rate once and every ticket adds it.

    Can my AI assistant set it up for me?

    If you use Claude or ChatGPT, it can add your vendors, load your inventory and get your store ready, with your permission. Add a custom connector with this URL:
