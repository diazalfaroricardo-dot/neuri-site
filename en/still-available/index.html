    Questions

    Do I need to be tech-savvy?

    No. You fill out the sign-up and connect your Facebook Page or WhatsApp in one click; we walk you through it. We build the rest.

    How does it work with Marketplace?

    You add your WhatsApp link to each listing and the assistant answers there. It also works on your Page's Messenger and Instagram.

    How do I update my items?

    In your catalog sheet. Mark an item as sold and the assistant stops offering it within about 10 minutes. How to publish your sheet.

    What if a buyer asks something it doesn't know?

    It doesn't make things up. It tells the buyer you'll answer and passes you the conversation on WhatsApp.

    Will it tell buyers my lowest price?

    Never. It only uses it to know how far it can negotiate, and only if you let it.

    How long does setup take?

    Usually 1 to 2 days. We send you a link to try it, and it doesn't answer anyone until you approve it.

    Can I turn it off anytime?

    Yes. Pause it whenever you want. There's no contract or minimum term, and you won't be charged the next month.

    How does billing work?

    Monthly by card. If a payment fails we let you know, and you have 5 days to update your card before it pauses. Nothing gets deleted.

    What buyer data do you keep?

    Only name and conversation, to answer and hold items. We never sell or share it.

    Is anyone using it yet?

    Not yet: we're looking for our first 10 sellers. That's why setup and the first month are free for them, no card needed. In return, we ask for your honest feedback.
