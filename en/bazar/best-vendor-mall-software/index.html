What a booth-rental mall needs

A regular POS takes payments. A vendor mall also has to know whose item it is and what each vendor is owed. Check that the software does these four things:

  • Rent that collects itself. Weekly or monthly, out of the vendor's balance, without chasing anyone.
  • Every sale tied to its vendor. The tag carries the booth number and the register adds the sale to that account.
  • A statement for each vendor. Sales, rent and balance, visible to the vendor without asking you.
  • Clear payouts. Know what you owe each vendor and have the file ready for the bank.

Common questions

How much does vendor mall software cost?

Among the ones we compared, US$79 to US$379 a month (checked November 26, 2026): neuri.bazar from US$79, Ricochet from US$99, ConsignCloud from US$139 and SimpleConsign from US$159; its booth-rental plan, Vendor Mall Plus, is US$379 with a US$199 intro price. ConsignPro is sold as a one-time license: ConsignCloud says US$1,495, which we could not confirm on ConsignPro's own site.

Which software collects booth rent automatically?

neuri.bazar, SimpleConsign Vendor Mall Plus, ConsignCloud and Ricochet all say on their sites that they take rent out of the vendor's balance or payout. SimpleConsign can also charge a card on file. We could not confirm it for ConsignPro.

Is there vendor mall software in Spanish?

Yes. neuri.bazar runs fully in English and Spanish, including each vendor's portal. The pricing pages of SimpleConsign, ConsignCloud and Ricochet do not mention Spanish (checked November 26, 2026).

Can I use Square or a regular POS for a vendor mall?

To take payments, yes. But Square for Retail's pricing page does not mention consignment or booth rent, so you would track each vendor's sales, rent and payout separately. Square for Retail costs US$0, US$49 or US$149 a month per location, plus 2.4% to 2.6% + 15¢ per in-person card payment.

How can vendors see their sales without asking me?

Through a vendor portal. In neuri.bazar it is called My Booth and opens in the phone's browser. SimpleConsign Vendor Mall Plus, ConsignCloud Pro (up to 1,000 vendors) and Ricochet also give each vendor a login to check sales or add items.

Do they take a commission on sales?

neuri.bazar takes no commission per sale and you keep your own card terminal. The others charge a monthly price; if you use their integrated payments, you pay each one's card rate. Ricochet publishes its rate: 2.69% to 3.29% + 10¢ per transaction with RicochetPay, depending on the plan.
