FAQ

Do I need new hardware?

No. It runs on the phones and tablets you already have. USB or Bluetooth scanner guns and label printers work too.

Do vendors have to install anything?

No. They open their private link to see balance, sales and items.

Do you handle sales tax?

Yes. Set your local rate; tax is added at checkout and reported separately from your commission.

How do vendor payouts work?

Neuri tracks each vendor’s balance (sales minus commission and rent). You pay by check, cash or transfer and record it; the vendor sees it instantly.

Can I bring my data from another system?

Yes. We import your vendors and items, and your existing tags keep scanning.
