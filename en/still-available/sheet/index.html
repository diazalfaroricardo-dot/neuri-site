How to publish your catalog sheet

Your assistant reads your Google Sheet to know which items are still for sale. You only need to publish it once (2 minutes).

  1. 1Open your sheet in Google Sheets

    Use the catalog sheet we sent you (or your own with item, price and sold columns).

  2. 2File → Share → Publish to web

    On a computer it's at the top left. On a phone, open the sheet in the browser in desktop mode.

    FileEditViewInsert
    New
    Share ▸ Publish to web
    Download
  3. 3Pick your catalog tab and “Comma-separated values (.csv)”

    That's the format the assistant can read. Keep “republish when changes are made” checked.

    CatalogComma-separated values (.csv)
  4. 4Click “Publish” and copy the link

    It should end in output=csv. Paste it in the sign-up form or reply to our email with it.

    Publish

    https://docs.google.com/…/pub?output=csv

Done. When you mark an item as sold in your sheet, the assistant stops offering it within minutes. Publishing only lets the table be read; nobody can edit it. If you switch sheets, repeat these steps and send us the new link.
