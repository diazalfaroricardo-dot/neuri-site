05 · Design principles

Autonomous. Honest. You're in charge.

01

Autonomous

Each agent finishes the whole job: the item held, the day closed, not just a suggestion.

02

Honest

It only answers with your information. If it doesn't know, it says so and passes the conversation to you.

03

You're in charge

Nothing answers until you switch it on. You decide what it handles alone, pause it any time and take your data with you.
