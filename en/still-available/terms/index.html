2. The service

An assistant that answers your customers’ messages on Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram (with the bot you create in @BotFather) and your website chat (the page sd.neuri.tech/c/<your business> or the button you paste on your site) on your behalf: whether an item is still available, prices, delivery and payment options; it holds items with a waitlist, books appointments and takes orders (depending on what you turn on), and tells you by email and in “My assistant” what you need to handle yourself. WhatsApp can already be connected from “My assistant”. TikTok and text messages (SMS) are being turned on in stages: we’ll let you know when they’re available for your business (SMS: section 17).

  • Connecting Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp depends on Meta’s approval, and TikTok on TikTok’s. Until it’s granted, the assistant can’t reply on that network.
  • The assistant answers with what you give us: your catalog or menu sheet, hours, delivery, payments and answers. What isn’t there it hands to you, and the prices it sends are checked against your data before they go out. What fixed rules can’t handle is written by Anthropic’s Claude model using your data.
  • By default, the first reply to each person says it’s your automated assistant; your customers can always ask to talk to you.
  • Neuri is a software provider. We are not a party to the sales, appointments or orders between your business and your customers, and we don’t receive their payments.
  • If the assistant emails your customers, the email may carry a small “Made with Neuri” line at the bottom with a link to neuri.tech.

Telegram, the website chat and text messages count as one account per channel: one Telegram bot, one website chat and one text (SMS) number per business.

17. Text messages (SMS)

Applies once text messaging is available for your business. In the US, messages are sent only through Neuri LLC's registered campaign (A2P 10DLC).

  • What it is: your business's assistant replies by SMS to people who text your business number (a number Neuri rents for you, or your own number hosted on Twilio): availability, prices, holds, appointments, orders and notices about that same conversation, such as an appointment confirmation or reminder. This is customer care: it does not send promotions or marketing.
  • Consent: a person gives consent by texting your number first. The assistant never sends the first message, with one exception: if you turn on “Missed call,” someone who called your number and wasn't answered gets one text asking how you can help (at most once every 24 hours), and is not texted again if they don't reply. You may not upload lists of numbers or use the service for bulk messaging.
  • Frequency: Message frequency varies: each reply answers a message from the person, plus notices about their appointment or order.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply, depending on the person's mobile plan.
  • Opt-out: the person can reply STOP at any time (also UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, BAJA or ALTO) to stop receiving messages from that number. They get one final confirmation and are not texted again unless they reply START.
  • Help: replying HELP sends information about who is texting and how to opt out. They can also email us at hola@neuri.tech.
  • Carriers: carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Privacy: mobile phone numbers and text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Details in the Privacy Policy.
  • What the business is responsible for: complying with US text messaging law (TCPA) and carrier rules (CTIA); not asking the assistant to text anyone who hasn't consented, and not sending promotions by SMS.
