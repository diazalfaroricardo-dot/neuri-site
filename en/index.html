How it works

Runs on the phones you already have. You can be set up in an afternoon.

  1. Tag the item

    Add the item with its price and vendor, and print a barcode tag.

  2. Ring it up

    Scan the tag with the phone camera. Sales tax, your commission and the vendor's share are split automatically.

  3. Pay each vendor

    Each vendor sees their sales and balance from their link. You pay them, record it, and their balance goes to zero.

Questions

Do I need new hardware?

No. It runs on the phones and tablets you already have. Scanner guns and label printers work too.

Do vendors have to install anything?

No. They open their private link to see their balance, sales and items.

Do you handle sales tax?

Yes. Set your local rate; tax is added at checkout and reported apart from your commission.

Can I bring my data from another system?

Yes. We import your vendors and items from a spreadsheet, and the tags already on your items keep scanning.

Is it in Spanish too?

Yes. Each person picks English or Spanish, so mixed staff and vendors each read it in their language.

What happens after the 14 days?

We let you know before it ends. If you stay, you pick a plan; your data stays as it is.
