An assessment built on real data Early access

We see where the hours go, we don't guess.

For 5 to 10 workdays, team members who agree to take part install Neuri's Chrome extension. It measures which apps people work in, which kinds of actions repeat and how long they take. That shows us which tasks are worth automating first and how many hours they free up.

  • Installs in a minuteEach person connects their browser with a code. Nothing to install on servers, no access to your systems.
  • Detects patterns, doesn't spyRecords the app, the kind of action and the time. Never what's typed, copied or pasted, and no titles, files or screenshots.
  • Your team stays in controlExplicit consent, one-click pause, blocked sites (banking, health, personal email) and nothing in incognito.
  • Data with an expiry dateDetailed records are deleted 30 days after the assessment is delivered. Only the summary remains.
