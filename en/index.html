        Example: how it would work in a 150-booth antique mall

        Monthly booth rent, showcases at a different commission, sales tax on top

        In a vendor mall every booth pays rent, some showcases take a different cut, and every month someone has to send out 150 statements and cut 150 checks.

        1. The 1st of the monthBooth rent posts on its own, and every vendor gets their monthly statement by email.
        2. Every dayRing up sales in dollars with sales tax added on top. Showcase items can carry their own commission.
        3. Your staffCashiers don't see costs. Managers approve discounts with their PIN. Everyone uses the app in English or Spanish.
        4. Payday and year endPay vendors by ACH with the bank file, or write checks and log the check number. In January, download what you paid each vendor for the year, ready for 1099s.

        Example: how it would work in a 40-vendor weekend market

          Questions we get

          Do I need to buy hardware?

          No. It runs on the phone you already have: the camera reads the tags. If you want to go faster, you can connect a handheld barcode scanner and a tag or receipt printer.

          My vendors aren't tech people. Do they have to install anything?

          No. Each vendor gets a private link, no password. They open it like any other link and see their balance, their items and their sales for the month. Their statement arrives by email.

          I run everything on paper or a spreadsheet today. Is it hard to switch?

          You upload your vendor list and your items from Excel or a CSV, and the tags already on your merchandise keep scanning. For our first 10 stores, we set it up and train your staff for free.

          What if the internet goes down?

          For now the register needs a connection. It works on cellular data, so a phone with a data plan or a hotspot is enough. Offline mode isn't ready yet.

          Does it file my sales tax or send 1099s?

          No, but it gets you the numbers. Sales tax is added on top of every sale and shows up in a monthly tax report for your filing, and at year end you download the total paid to each vendor, ready for your 1099s. You or your accountant file them.

          Do you take a cut of every sale?

          No. You pay one flat monthly price for your store, no matter how much you sell.

          What happens when the 14-day free trial ends?

          We email you two days before. If you stay, you pick a plan and everything stays as it is. If not, you aren't charged anything.

          Is my data mine?

          Yes. Download your whole business with one click, any time. And every change to a price or commission, and every voided sale, goes into an activity log that no one can edit or delete, not even the owner.

          Does it work in Spanish, or in Mexico?

          Yes. Each user picks English or Spanish. In Mexico it runs in pesos with IVA included.
