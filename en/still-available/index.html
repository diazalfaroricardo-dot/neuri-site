Questions

What almost everyone asks before starting.

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

No. You fill out a 3-minute form and connect your Facebook Page with one click; we walk you through it. We build everything else.

What if the assistant doesn't know an answer?

It never makes things up. It hands the chat to you and pings you so you can reply.

Will it reveal my lowest price?

Never. Your floor price only tells the assistant how far it can go if you allow it; buyers never see it.

How do I update my items?

In your catalog sheet: mark an item as sold and the assistant stops offering it within minutes. No need to message us.

How long until it's live?

Usually the same day you connect your Page.

Can I turn it off anytime?

Yes. Pause it whenever you want. No contract; cancel anytime and you won't be charged the next month.

How does billing work?

Monthly, by card. If a payment fails we let you know and you have 5 days to update your card before the assistant pauses. Nothing gets deleted.

What customer data do you keep?

Only what's needed to answer and hold items (name and the conversation). We never sell or share data.

Does it work with Marketplace and Instagram?

Yes. Marketplace buyers message your Page on Messenger and the assistant answers there. It also works on Instagram DMs and WhatsApp.

How do I know how it's going?

Every Monday you get a summary: conversations handled, visits booked and items held.
