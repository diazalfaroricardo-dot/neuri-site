neuri.bazar in 2 minutes

Real screens from the app, using a sample antique mall. Booths rent at a fixed monthly price; the mall rings up sales, and each vendor sees what they are owed.

  1. Booths and rent

    Every booth with its monthly rent, size, vendor and status: taken, free or on hold. Free up a booth or edit it in one tap.

  2. Add a whole row at once

    Setting up the mall? «Add several» takes a range like A1–A20, a list, or your Excel/CSV file with number, rent and size.

  3. The register

    Find an item by code or name; it shows which vendor and booth it belongs to. Take cash or card (with your terminal reference), add a discount or a gift card.

  4. Vendor payouts

    See what you owe each vendor, generate a file to upload to your bank's portal, or record that you paid another way.

  5. Your vendors

    Everyone renting a booth, in one list.

  6. My Booth, for each vendor

    Vendors open their own page: what they are owed, when they get paid, their next rent, their sales, items and statements. Fewer «did anything sell?» calls.

  7. A page to rent a booth

    A public page shows how many booths are open and the rent, and takes requests to rent one; the applicant confirms their email.

  8. A poster for the front door

    Print a letter-size poster with a QR code that opens that page.
