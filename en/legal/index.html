This site (neuri.tech)

neuri.tech pages have no accounts or payments of their own. When you visit them:

  • Anonymous analytics with PostHog (US servers): counts visits and button clicks with no cookies or browser storage, no session recording and no names, emails or phone numbers. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, it stays off.
  • Google Fonts: some pages download fonts from Google servers, which receive your IP address when serving them.
  • Your language: if you pick English or Español, we keep that choice only in your browser (local storage) to remember it. It is not a cookie and you can clear it from your browser.
  • AI assistant: when the help bubble shows up, what you type is processed with Claude (Anthropic) as the neuri.consulting privacy policy explains. If it doesn’t show up, it is off and nothing is sent.
  • Sign-up or waitlist forms: what you send is kept by the product you sign up for, under its policy above.
  • Hosting: the site is served from GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security.

How we protect your information

  • Everything travels encrypted: neuri.tech and the apps use HTTPS; bazar.neuri.tech and consulting.neuri.tech make your browser always use it (HSTS) and can’t be loaded inside other pages.
  • Payments through Stripe: charges are processed by Stripe. Neuri never receives or stores your full card number.
  • Your data, under your control: each privacy policy above says what that product keeps, for how long and how to ask for it to be deleted.
  • Found a security issue? Email hola@neuri.tech. It’s also in security.txt.
