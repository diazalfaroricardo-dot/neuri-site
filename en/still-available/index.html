What it does and never does

It answers

  • Whether the item is still available
  • Price, size and condition
  • Where you are, hours and delivery

  • Holds items and books pickups, then lets you know

It never

  • Reveals your lowest price
  • Makes things up: what it doesn't know goes to you
  • Offers items you marked as sold
  • Sends ads to your buyers

Questions

Do I need to be tech-savvy?

No. You fill out the sign-up and connect your Facebook Page or WhatsApp with one click; we walk you through it. We build the rest.

How does it work with Marketplace?

Marketplace buyers message your Page on Messenger and the assistant answers there. It also works on Instagram DMs and WhatsApp.

How do I update my items?

In your catalog sheet: mark an item as sold and it stops offering it within minutes. How to publish your sheet.

Can I turn it off anytime?

Yes. Pause it whenever you want and cancel with no contract; you won't be charged the next month.

How does billing work?

Monthly by card. If a payment fails we let you know and you have 5 days to update it before it pauses. Nothing gets deleted.

What buyer data do you keep?

Only name and conversation, to answer and hold items. We never sell or share it.
