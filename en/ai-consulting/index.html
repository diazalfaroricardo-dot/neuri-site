Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be technical?

No. We handle the technical side and explain every decision in plain language.

How much does it cost?

The AI Score is free. The diagnostic starts at $249 USD / $4,500 MXN depending on the size of your business. The implementation plan is $399 USD / $7,200 MXN. Implementation is quoted at a fixed price per phase, and the diagnostic fee is credited if you go ahead.

Do you work with businesses in both the US and Mexico?

Yes. We work remotely in both countries, in English or Spanish, and on-site in the Phoenix area when a project needs it.

Which tools do you use?

The ones you already pay for, first. If something new is needed, we propose options with their monthly cost and start on free or basic plans where possible.

I only need help with one specific problem. Is that a fit?

Yes. Tell us what's happening; often it's solved in one or two support sessions.
