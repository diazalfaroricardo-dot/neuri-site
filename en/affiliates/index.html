How it works

Three steps, no fine print.

  1. Apply and sign up

    With a verified email and a Stripe Connect Express account, which is how payouts are sent.

  2. Share your link

    Add ?ref=your-code to any page on neuri.tech or the apps. If the person signs up within 60 days, it counts for you; the last link they opened wins.

  3. Get paid monthly

    Each payment becomes an earned commission 30 days after it is collected. Every month a batch is approved by Neuri and paid through Stripe Connect from US$25 or MX$500.

Terms

The full terms.

These are the terms the program would open with. Neuri may adjust them before turning it on; the version in force will be the one published here.

Commission

  • 20 % of what the customer pays during their first 12 months, counted from their first payment, for as long as they keep paying.
  • Covers neuri.bazar, neuri.bots, neuri.consulting and Neuri Marketing.
  • For neuri.consulting, the diagnostic and the monthly plan both count.

Attribution

  • A ?ref=your-code link on any page of neuri.tech or the apps.
  • Last click wins, within a 60-day window before sign-up.
  • The code is saved to the customer's account when they sign up.

When it is earned

  • Each payment stays pending and becomes an earned commission 30 days after it is collected, to cover refunds.
  • A refund or chargeback reverses that payment's commission; if it was already paid, it is deducted from the next batch.

Payout

  • Each month a summary is built per affiliate, and Neuri approves the batch before any money moves.
  • Payouts are sent through Stripe Connect Express.
  • Minimum payout: US$25 / MX$500. Anything below the minimum rolls over to the next month.

Not allowed

  • Referring yourself: same email, same card or same company domain.
  • Commissions on Neuri's own accounts.
  • Bidding on the «Neuri» brand in ads.

Disclosure and data

  • You must disclose the relationship when you share your link, for example with «#ad» (FTC / PROFECO).
  • Your dashboard shows no personal customer data: only product, plan, amount and status.

FAQ

Common questions.

When does the program open?

There is no date yet. It opens when Neuri turns it on; until then, no applications are accepted and no clicks are recorded.

Do I need to be a Neuri customer?

No. Anyone with a verified email and a Stripe Connect Express account will be able to apply.

Can I use my own link for my account?

No. Self-referrals are blocked: the same email, the same card or the same company domain earn no commission.

What if the customer cancels?

You stop earning from that point. What you already earned stays, unless that payment is refunded or charged back.

Is this the same as neuri.bots «refer a friend»?

No. That program gives free months between customers and stays as it is. This one is for affiliates and pays in money.
