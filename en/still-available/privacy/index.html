Still Available? privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026. Still Available? (¿Sigue Disponible?) is a service of Neuri LLC (Phoenix, Arizona) that answers, on behalf of a business, buyers' questions on Instagram and Messenger about whether an item is still for sale.

What data we receive from Instagram and Messenger

  • The text of messages a buyer sends to the business and the replies sent back (we keep up to 1,000 characters per message).
  • The sender ID assigned by Meta. We use it to reply; in our message log it is stored as a one-way hash, not the original ID.
  • The profile name, only if Meta includes it with the message.
  • For a business that connects its account: its Facebook Page or Instagram account ID and the access token Meta provides, stored encrypted.

Why we use it

Only to answer, on behalf of the business, questions about availability, price, location and appointments for its items, and to give the business reports on how many messages were handled. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

The message log is deleted automatically after 90 days. If the business cancels and asks us to delete its data, or Meta tells us a user asked for deletion, it is deleted sooner (see below).

Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms (Instagram and Messenger), which delivers and sends the messages.
  • Anthropic, as a processor, when its Claude model is used to draft a reply; it receives the text needed to answer.
  • Our hosting provider (Railway), where the service runs and the data is stored.

We don't share it with anyone else unless required by law.

How to request deletion

You can remove the app from your Facebook or Instagram settings (Settings → Apps and websites); Meta sends us the request and we delete the messages tied to your account and, if you connected your business account, that connection and its token. You get a confirmation code and a page to check the status. You can also email hola@neuri.tech and we'll do it by hand.
